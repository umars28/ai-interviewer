Pull the specific facts out of one interview answer. Do nothing else.

Question asked:
{question}

Answer given:
{answer}

Go through the answer clause by clause. For each specific thing stated, write one short
standalone sentence: an action taken, a duration, a count, a date, a tool named, a decision
made, a consequence. Keep the respondent's own terms. Do not interpret, summarise, judge,
or combine two statements into one.

Worked example. Answer: "It was two days before the deadline, and I was trying to find the
file. I checked the folders, the cloud storage, everything. I spent about forty minutes
before I gave up."

- The deadline was two days away when the file went missing.
- She checked the folders and the cloud storage.
- She searched for about forty minutes before giving up.

If the answer is genuinely unanchored — "a while", "pretty often", "it was annoying", with
no occasion, number, or action attached — return an empty list. Do not invent specificity
the answer does not contain.
