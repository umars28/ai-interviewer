learned from each respondent.

Research goal:
{research_goal}

Produce 4 to 6 goals. Each goal is one thing you need to find out, written as a short
question to yourself — not as a question you would read aloud to a respondent.

Rules:
- Each goal must be about something the respondent has actually done or experienced.
  Reject anything that can only be answered by speculation about the future.
- Goals must be distinct. If two goals would be satisfied by the same answer, merge them.
- Order them so that concrete, easy-to-recall material comes first and reflective or
  sensitive material comes last.
- Use ids of the form g1, g2, g3.
- Leave every status as untouched.
